Your composition

Real pitches in treble clef, written as a one-handed jazz line you can play on a piano. While you focus, the score holds a slow, quiet theme (ppp), silent in the room. As you get more distracted the tempo and volume rise and the scale gets looser: ballad (Dorian, 60 bpm), swing (blues, 84), bebop (116) and frantic chromatic runs (150), pp to ff, in the key you picked before the session. Colour is your state. Press play, or click anywhere on the score to start from there. Change the speed with the menu next to the button.
